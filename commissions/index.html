2025Public commission

If We Don’t, Who Will?

More Art · 300 Ashland Place, Downtown Brooklyn

A performative and functional AI laboratory housed in an upcycled shipping container designed with LOT-EK. The commission invited the public to contribute self-determined stories to a living, community-rooted dataset.

2022–26Hewlett 50 Arts Commission

Data Trust / Data Trust Forest

Institute of Contemporary Art San José · San José, California

A commission combining storytelling traditions with emergent AI and DNA-storage technologies. Community narratives become a living archive held through organic matter, soil, okra, and California black oak—transforming conversations about the past into more enduring futures.

University of Michigan · Purdue · University of Maryland · Georgia Tech · Stony Brook
